W3.2 DECISION SCREEN — REPRODUCTION
===================================

Command (from the repository root, no arguments beyond the output path):

    python3 cad/decision_screen.py --output evidence/week-2026-09-19/decision_screen

Environment: python3 only, standard library. No solver, no device, no network. The screen imports the
frozen campaign estimator (experiments/mast_physical_validation.py::_linear_fit) rather than
reimplementing it, so a change to the campaign's own fitting code changes these numbers too.

Determinism: seed 20260925 is fixed in the source. 1000 trials per grid point, 5000 for the
decision-boundary and intervention cases. No seed was selected for a favourable outcome; the seed and
trial count are recorded in scenarios.json next to every result.

WHAT THIS IS
------------
A conditional sensitivity study over ASSUMED terms. Its purpose is to rank feasible interventions by
how much they reduce campaign rejection risk, so the three input requests ask for the evidence that
would actually change a decision.

WHAT THIS IS NOT
----------------
Not a measurement. Not a purchase specification. Not an approved threshold. Not a complete uncertainty
budget. Every physical term is assumed, no instrument has been characterised, and no fixture exists.

The authoritative state of the project is scenario S0: the screen REFUSES with UNKNOWN because no
fixture stiffness is declared on either axis. Every other scenario adds an assumed 8000 N/mm purely to
make the axes simulable. None of them replaces S0.

WHY PASS FRACTIONS REPLACED THE MEDIAN
--------------------------------------
A passing median is not campaign reliability. Reproduced here at 5000 trials:

    assumed repeatability   median R^2   per-axis pass   both-axes pass
    0.5 micrometres         0.986027     0.1270          0.0162
    0.3 micrometres         0.992519     0.8602          0.7246

At 0.3 um the median passes the frozen R^2 >= 0.99 rule while about one campaign in seven is rejected
on a single axis, and rather more than one in four fails on at least one of the two. The screen now
gates on the simulated pass fraction, not the median.

The Monte Carlo standard errors are about 0.005. They express simulation sampling precision ONLY. They
say nothing about whether the assumed physical model is correct.

The two-axis figure is simulated with the shared force-calibration gain drawn ONCE per campaign and
reused on both axes, with readout, repeatability and rounding drawn per channel. The product of the two
per-axis fractions is deliberately not reported: that would assume an independence the setup does not
have, since one calibration and one environment serve both axes.

SCOPE OF THE SIMULATED GATES
----------------------------
scenarios.json records gate_scope. The screen simulates the fixture-stiffness, relative-U95, R^2,
indicator and dimension gates. It marks the frozen hysteresis and FEA-agreement gates not_evaluated,
because it does not simulate loading/unloading hysteresis and no as-built reference exists. Passing the
simulated subset is NOT passing every campaign gate.

THE RESULT THAT MATTERS MOST
----------------------------
Scenario S4_rotation_adverse applies a 0.00025 mm/N uncorrected, load-proportional root rotation,
explicitly synthetic. It yields a 19.4 % slope bias while the median R^2 is 0.9947 and the relative U95
is 3.3 %. Both frozen numerical gates pass and the answer is still 19 % wrong. Low scatter and good
linearity do not establish an accurate compliance estimate, and no amount of repetition detects this.
