Key engineering decisions
-------------------------

  - Use the 5 kg cell for the planned 4/8/12/16/20 N force range. With the repo's
    g=9.81 m/s² convention, its nominal capacity is 49.05 N; 20 N corresponds
    to 2.039 kg equivalent. Check total cell loading including tare and mounting.
  - The 1 kg cell's nominal capacity is 9.81 N. It cannot cover 12/16/20 N and
    must never be silently substituted into the full campaign profile. Use it
    only for a separately bounded low-range exercise with mounting verified.
  - The nominal FEA predicts approximately 27.3 micrometres displacement at
    20 N. The LIS3DH cannot replace calibrated tip/root displacement indicators
    by double-integrating acceleration. Root rotation also needs observation.
  - NAU7802 tops out at 320 samples/s. Its 160 Hz Nyquist limit is below the
    selected nominal 285.5 Hz mode. This force chain cannot establish a valid
    force-to-acceleration response/coherence measurement at that mode.
  - LIS3DH bandwidth depends on operating mode, not just the advertised sample
    rate. Keep any vibration results exploratory until timing, filtering,
    mounting, sensor mass, calibration, and the modal protocol are resolved.
  - The 128.76 N crash-design load exceeds both cells' nominal force capacities;
    the 50 g design case also exceeds the LIS3DH's ±16 g maximum range. These
    design cases are not test instructions for this purchased equipment.
