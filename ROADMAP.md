# Roadmap

This is the plan for finishing the project. Task-level CAD records live in
[docs/CAD_TASKS.csv](docs/CAD_TASKS.csv); older sprint history is in
[docs/SPRINT_PROGRESS.md](docs/SPRINT_PROGRESS.md) and
[docs/REVIEW_READY.md](docs/REVIEW_READY.md).

## Finish line

The owner must settle the mounting need and intended claim before fabrication.
The delegated assessment starts with the lowest rigid mount that preserves
the required scan field. Its final height and design remain unapproved.

- If the owner chooses a deck mount and ends mast work, close the mast as a
  design study with the corrected result in the README and resume.
- If a raised mast is required, finish with a measured assembly response for
  the selected claim. Modal characterization needs a qualified measurement
  chain and tests in both bending directions, including repeated taps and
  remounts. Suitability for racing also needs measured excitation and an
  allowed scan error.

The vehicle software remains a completed simulation study. Identification
checks consistency with telemetry generated by the same model. Keep the
existing software results reproducible.

## Where it stands

- Vehicle software: see the [integrated report](reports/final_report.md).
- Mast design: the original frequency was a
  [hand calculation](runs/mast_hand_calc/summary.txt). The redesign has
  [hand calculations](runs/mast_hand_calc/design_sweep.txt) and
  [FEA output](runs/mast_fea/fea_summary.txt).
- The [tip-mass attachment study](runs/mast_modal_attachment_20260925/study.json)
  explains much of the hand/FEA gap. Its coupled, centred model remains
  unconverged; the historical model and results are preserved.
- The [drivetrain assessment](docs/design/16_mechanical_design_analysis.md#61-retrospective-assessment--is-the-200-hz-guard-the-right-criterion-audit-f1)
  shows shaft order crossing the predicted mast mode within the operating
  speed range. The historical frequency threshold is under review because
  it does not establish motor-vibration clearance. The crossing alone does
  not establish harmful vibration; the excitation amplitude and response
  remain unknown.
- CAD: tube, support sleeve and root clamp are parametric SOLIDWORKS parts.
  The assembly was accepted against an independent CadQuery model
  ([#55](https://github.com/500ft/autonomous-racing-systems/pull/55)).
- Test tooling exists for static compliance, load-cell calibration and bench
  logging, with synthetic tests. There are no physical measurements.

## What's left

| # | Step | Who | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Resolve mounting and study scope before fabrication. Confirm the required scan field, obstruction, mounting envelope, and sensor mass and centre of mass. Choose closure as a design study or the claim a raised assembly must support. | Owner, informed by the delegated low-mount assessment | Mounting need and scope are recorded. **Current step.** |
| 2 | If continuing, qualify the measurement chain for the selected assembly modes and freeze the test protocol and both model predictions before measuring. | Owner and agent | Bandwidth, noise, clipping, mounting mass and test acceptance support the selected claim |
| 3 | Measure the actual assembly in both bending directions with repeated taps and remounts; compare with both frozen predictions. | Owner, with agent analysis | Response and uncertainty are reported; either or both predictions may be rejected. A racing claim also addresses excitation and scan error. |
| 4 | Report the chosen outcome in the report, README and portfolio, or record closure as a design study. | Agent | Reviewed changes merged |

The [existing static stiffness protocol](docs/specs/mast-physical-validation/design.md)
remains an optional study and must account for root rotation. Its verdict
cannot establish modal or vibration performance. Prior fixture and fabrication
tasks wait for the mounting and scope decision.

Re-converging the coupled-mass model is optional and requires separate
authorization. No new frequency threshold is selected here.
