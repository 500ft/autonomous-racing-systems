# Roadmap

## Question and finish line

Can disagreement among dynamics models consistent with development data tell
when a simulated controller ranking should be withheld?

The owner adopted this question and authorized the structural qualification
implemented here. This task finishes with reproducible archive, loader and
policy-correspondence findings, clear estimator eligibility and a reviewable
repository update. It does not authorize an empirical ranking campaign.

A later study would compare rank error and the fraction of comparisons retained
at matched coverage against nominal simulation, whole-run uncertainty,
state-action coverage and eligible FQE. Any such study needs a separately
frozen protocol and authorization after the prerequisites below qualify.
A simple coverage baseline explaining all benefit would defeat a claim of
added value from model disagreement. No performance result exists yet.
